# Registrations — the ones only a person can do

Eight relationship rows now sit on the tracker board next to real jobs. Five of
them are a form somebody has to fill in. No collector can do these; they are
here so they stop being invisible.

Ordered by leverage, not by effort.

---

## 1. COMPASS — the single highest-leverage action on this list

**https://compass.bespokemetrics.com** · due 2026-09-26

Moss is the GC for Water Street (Cora, and the entertainment-venue JV with
Barton Malow) **and** for Gasworx (the office building and Olivette). Moss
prequalifies subcontractors through COMPASS, and so do more than 60 other
Florida GCs. One registration opens all of them.

Register **Ideal Construction** and complete the qualification statement. Have
ready:

- CGC1537480 and MRSR5016
- Certificate of insurance — GL, auto, workers' comp, umbrella
- Bonding capacity letter from the surety, single and aggregate
- Three years of financial statements
- EMR and OSHA 300 logs for three years
- Five reference projects with owner and GC contacts

Under work performed, enter what we actually self-perform and what we manage as
GC. Do not overstate scope — COMPASS scores completeness and consistency, and
a GC reads the same record every time they consider us.

## 2. AECOM Hunt and Turner — the Rays stadium district

**https://www.aecom.com/suppliers/** and
**https://www.turnerconstruction.com/subcontractors** · due 2026-10-03

Both were named construction manager for the stadium district. Hillsborough
County approved the deal on **28 Aug 2026**: site prep started September 2026,
demolition December, foundations March 2027, Opening Day 2029, inside a
113-acre mixed-use district on the Hillsborough College Dale Mabry site.

The bowl itself is out of reach. The retail and F&B fitouts around it are not,
and they are bought out years before they open. Register with both — they run
separate lists — and ask specifically about the district's commercial fitout
packages rather than the stadium scope.

The `dalemabry` submarket polygon is drawn on this site, so the collector is
already returning permits there. Anything it finds will land in the tray.

## 3. Tampa General Hospital — vendor / supplier application

**https://www.tgh.org/vendor--supplier-information/** · due 2026-09-30

Healthcare is the proven niche, and the Taneja tower will pull specialist
practices, imaging and dental-adjacent offices into Davis Islands, Hyde Park
and Bayshore for years after it opens. TGH runs a formal vendor and supplier
application with its own certification requirements.

Submit it and **ask explicitly for the construction-services category** — the
default routing is materials and clinical supply, which is not us.

## 4. HCAA / Tampa International — catch the next cycle

**https://procurement.opengov.com/portal/tampaairport** · due 2026-09-19

The Prequalified Contractors List RFP — building construction, site work,
paving — **closed 3 Sep 2026**. We missed it. Two things to do:

1. Email **Nick Diaz, ndiaz@tampaairport.com**, asking when the next
   prequalification cycle opens and whether an interim list is maintained.
2. Register on **OpenGov** (link above) and on **DemandStar** for HCAA so the
   next solicitation arrives by email rather than by luck.

Concession fitout contractors are governed by the Tenant Work Permit Handbook,
which is a separate track from the prequalified-contractor list — worth asking
Diaz about both.

The monthly Planned Procurement Opportunities report is the standing watch
item; a collector for it is not built yet (PLAN.md §3 #6d).

## 5. BuildingConnected and PlanHub — finish the profiles

**https://app.buildingconnected.com** · due 2026-09-26

BuildingConnected invitations are driven by two fields, and an incomplete
profile is invisible to every GC using it:

- **Work Performed** — tenant improvement, medical office, restaurant, retail
- **Service Area** — Hillsborough, Pinellas, Pasco

Set the same on PlanHub. `planhub_gc_scraper.py` in this repo already logs in
there, so the credentials exist.
